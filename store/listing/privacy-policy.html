Privacy Policy for Poker vs Dealer

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Short version: this app shows ads from Google AdMob. Depending on your consent choices, AdMob may use device identifiers to personalise ads. The app has no account system, and your game progress stays on your own device.

Poker vs Dealer ("we", "us") is a free-to-play card game using virtual chips with no cash value. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.micorlov.pokervsdealer.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier. There is no sign-in, no account, and no user profile stored on any server. The app's own code collects nothing. It shows ads from Google AdMob; depending on your consent choices, AdMob may use device identifiers to personalise ads (see Third Parties).

App Permissions

The app declares a small set of standard Android permissions that come from the application framework it is built with, including network access, vibration, and audio settings. Network access is used to load ads from Google AdMob; the vibration and audio permissions are used only for haptic feedback and sound effects on your device.

Data Stored On Your Device

To make the game playable, a small amount of data is saved locally using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Gameplay data: your virtual chip balance, win streak, and statistics such as hands played and win rate.
  • Preferences: settings such as sound.

Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Third Parties

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Depending on your consent choices, AdMob may use device identifiers to personalise ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not sell your data, and the app has no analytics libraries or social media integrations.

Advertising and Purchases

The app shows ads from Google AdMob and has no in-app purchases. Virtual chips cannot be bought, sold, cashed out, or exchanged for anything of value.

Children's Privacy

This game features simulated gambling and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. Ads are not intended for, and the app is not directed at, anyone under 18.
