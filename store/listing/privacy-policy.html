Privacy Policy for Poker vs Dealer

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Short version: this app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. It has no account system and contacts no server. Everything it saves stays on your own device.

Poker vs Dealer ("we", "us") is a free-to-play card game using virtual chips with no cash value. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data. It applies to the Android app with the package name com.micorlov.pokervsdealer.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier. There is no sign-in, no account, and no user profile stored on any server. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting code, and makes no network requests of any kind.

App Permissions

The app declares a small set of standard Android permissions that come from the application framework it is built with, including network access, vibration, and audio settings. The game's own code never opens a network connection and has no server to connect to; the vibration and audio permissions are used only for haptic feedback and sound effects on your device.

Data Stored On Your Device

To make the game playable, a small amount of data is saved locally using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Gameplay data: your virtual chip balance, win streak, and statistics such as hands played and win rate.
  • Preferences: settings such as sound.

Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit any data to third parties, because no data is collected in the first place. The app contains no analytics libraries, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no social media integrations.

Advertising and Purchases

The app contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases. Virtual chips cannot be bought, sold, cashed out, or exchanged for anything of value.

Children's Privacy

This game features simulated gambling and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.
